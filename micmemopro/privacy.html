MicMemo Pro privacy notice

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Short version

We don't run a server and we never receive your recordings or anything else about you. The app has no account of ours, no ads and no analytics. Your recordings stay on your watch unless you send them to your own Dropbox.

On the watch

Recordings, their names and your settings are kept in the app's storage on your watch. Recording and playback work with no phone and no internet. Deleting a recording or the app removes it from the watch.

Location

Save location is off until you turn it on. When it's on, the watch's own GPS saves where each recording was made, kept with the recording on your watch. It only leaves the watch if you send that recording to Dropbox (see below).

Sending to Dropbox

Only when you choose Send to Dropbox or Send all, the app sends recordings from your watch to your own Dropbox account, through the Garmin Connect app on your phone. We don't see them on the way.

  • You sign in on Dropbox's own page. The app never sees your Dropbox password. It keeps a sign-in token on the watch, which Settings > Dropbox > sign out removes.
  • The app can only write to its own folder in your Dropbox, Apps/MicMemo Pro. It can't read or change anything else there.
  • Each file is named after the recording's date, time and name. If the recording has a location, a small text file with its coordinates and links to Google Maps and OpenStreetMap is sent with it.
  • What happens in your Dropbox is covered by Dropbox's privacy policy.

Buying MicMemo Pro

Sending to Dropbox is unlocked with a one-time purchase, handled by KiezelPay. To show a purchase code and check whether sending is unlocked, the app talks to KiezelPay through the Garmin Connect app, sending an identifier for your watch and its model number. You pay on KiezelPay's website, and your payment details and email address go to KiezelPay and its payment providers, never to us. KiezelPay's own privacy policy covers that.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations vary. It's your responsibility to follow the rules where you are, including getting consent where that's required.
