Dog Clicker privacy notice

Last updated November 17, 2026.

Short version

We don't run a server, and the app sends us nothing: not your training sessions, nor anything else about you. The app has no account of ours, no ads and no analytics. Your sessions and settings stay on your watch. The one thing that leaves it is the trial and purchase check with KiezelPay, described below.

On the watch

Your training sessions (the commands you trained, the clicks for each, and when and how long you trained) and your settings are kept in the app's storage on your watch. Clicking, counting and the history work with no phone and no internet. Deleting a session from the history, or deleting the app, removes it from the watch.

Settings on your phone

You can also change the settings, such as your list of commands, in the Connect IQ app on your phone. They go between your phone and your watch through Garmin's apps, as for any Connect IQ app, and never reach us.

The trial and buying Dog Clicker

Dog Clicker is free for 3 days, then unlocked with a one-time purchase handled by KiezelPay. To start the trial, show a purchase code and check whether the app is unlocked, the app talks to KiezelPay through the Garmin Connect app on your phone, sending an identifier for your watch and its model number. It does this when it first starts, when the trial is over, and now and then after a purchase. That is the only reason the app asks for the internet (Communications) permission.

You pay on KiezelPay's website. Your payment details and email address go to KiezelPay and its payment providers, and KiezelPay's own privacy policy covers them.
